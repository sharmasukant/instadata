InstaData Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: August 11, 2026

How to Request Deletion

To request deletion of your Facebook or Instagram data from InstaData, email us at sharmasukant01@gmail.com with the subject line "InstaData Data Deletion Request".

What to Include

Please include the Facebook Page name, Instagram username, or account URL you want removed from InstaData.

What We Delete

We will remove stored account records, saved analytics, and Meta access tokens associated with your connected account where applicable.

Processing Time

We aim to process deletion requests within 7 business days.
