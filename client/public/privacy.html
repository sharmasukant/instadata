InstaData Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

Overview

InstaData is a social analytics tool used to view and manage analytics for connected social media accounts. This policy explains what information the app accesses and how it is used.

Information We Access

When you connect a Facebook or Instagram account, InstaData may access basic profile, Facebook Page, and linked Instagram Business or Creator account information through Meta APIs. This can include page IDs, account IDs, usernames, profile images, follower counts, post counts, engagement metrics, and access tokens required to call Meta APIs.

How We Use Information

We use this information only to display analytics in the InstaData dashboard, refresh account metrics, and maintain your connected account status.

Data Storage

In local development, connected account information and tokens are stored on the developer's server in local JSON files. In production, this data should be stored securely and access should be limited to authorized users.

Data Sharing

InstaData does not sell personal data. Data accessed through Meta APIs is used only for the app functionality described above.

Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your connected account data by following the instructions on our Data Deletion page.
